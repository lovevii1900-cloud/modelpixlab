What is Nano Banana 2?

Nano Banana 2 is Google's latest image generation and editing model (built on the Gemini image line). It became famous for one thing: editing real photos with plain-language instructions — change the background, relight the scene, swap an outfit — while keeping faces and details consistent.

What it's best at

  • Photo editing by sentence — "make it sunset", "remove the crowd", done.
  • Character consistency — the same face across many generated scenes.
  • Fast drafts — near-instant previews for product shots and social posts.
  • Text touch-ups — fix or add short text inside images.

Prompt ideas to try

  • "Product photo of a glass perfume bottle on wet stone, morning mist"
  • "Turn this selfie into a 90s film photo, warm grain"
  • "A tiny banana astronaut floating in a kitchen, Pixar style"

Nano Banana 2 FAQ

Is the Nano Banana 2 generator free?

You get free generations every day. Heavy use, HD output and no-watermark downloads are on paid plans.

Can it edit my own photos?

Yes — upload a photo and describe the change. Face-consistent editing is what this model is known for.

How fast is it?

Most images finish in about 10 seconds at standard resolution.
