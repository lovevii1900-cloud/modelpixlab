Editing guide

Edit photos with AI in plain words.

How to change backgrounds, remove objects, relight and restyle photos — no Photoshop needed.

Upload a photo, describe the change in one sentence, and the AI does the edit while keeping faces and details consistent. These are the five edits people use most, with prompts you can copy.

Workflows

The 5 edits everyone wants

EDIT 1

Swap the background

Upload your photo, then: "Replace the background with a Santorini sunset over the sea, keep the person exactly the same." Portraits, product shots and pet photos all work — lighting on the subject is matched automatically.

EDIT 2

Remove objects & people

"Remove the tourists in the background" or "erase the trash can on the left, fill in naturally." One object per round gives the cleanest result.

EDIT 3

Relight the scene

"Make it golden hour" or "change to moody blue night lighting." The fastest way to rescue a flat midday photo — faces stay recognizable.

EDIT 4

Change outfits & details

"Change her jacket to a red leather jacket" or "give him round glasses." Small, named changes beat vague ones like "make it cooler".

EDIT 5

Restyle the photo

"Turn this into a watercolor painting" or "make it look like a 1990s film photo." The composition stays, the medium changes.

Step by step

How an edit works

STEP 1

Upload your photo

Open the Nano Banana 2 page, switch to Edit mode and upload. JPG or PNG both work; bigger is better for detail.

STEP 2

Describe one change

Write what should be different, not the whole picture: "remove the car behind me". The AI keeps everything you did not mention.

STEP 3

Refine round by round

Each edit builds on the last result. Fix one thing per round — background first, then light, then details — and you stay in control.

Tips

Edits that come out clean

01

One change per round

"Remove the dog AND change the sky AND make it night" splits the model's attention. Stack edits one at a time.

02

Name what stays

"Keep the face exactly the same" protects what matters. The model preserves unmentioned areas, but naming them helps.

03

Start from the best photo

AI edits inherit the source quality. A sharp, well-lit original gives a sharp result; blur in, blur out.

04

Check hands and text

Regenerated hands and small text are the usual weak spots. Zoom in before downloading.

FAQ

Editing questions

Will AI editing change my face?
Nano Banana 2 is built to keep faces consistent across edits. Say "keep the face identical" in your prompt for extra safety, and always compare with the original before downloading.
What photo formats can I upload?
JPG and PNG work. Larger images keep more detail through the edit — phone photos are usually fine.
Can I undo an edit?
Each edit starts from your last result, so re-upload the original to start over. For multi-step edits, download the versions you like along the way.
