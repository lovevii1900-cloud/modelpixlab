Model guide

What is Canva Craft?

Canva Craft is Canva's first in-house AI image generation model, released on October 22, 2026. Built from scratch by Canva Research, it takes a design-first approach: instead of turning a prompt straight into pixels, it first plans the composition — layout, spacing, visual hierarchy, and any text — and only then renders the image. The result targets the assets Canva's users make every day: posters, social posts, presentations, infographics, and menus, with legible text built in rather than bolted on. It is available to all Canva users through the Dream Lab tab, and to developers through the Canva Connect API.

Key points

What to know about Canva Craft

01

Two-stage generation

Craft first writes a plan for the image — which elements appear, where they sit, their relative sizes, visual hierarchy, and the exact text — and only then renders pixels. Canva says this planning step is what keeps layouts and lettering intact.

02

Trained on designs, not photos

Instead of the photograph-heavy datasets behind most image models, Canva trained Craft on millions of human-made templates from its own library: posters, social posts, presentations, infographics, menus, and typography-rich layouts.

03

2K output, 30 aspect ratios

Craft renders images at up to 2K resolution in up to 30 aspect ratios, covering the formats used across social, web, print, and slides.

04

Text in 55 languages

The model can render text inside images in 55 languages — the headline use case for sale posters, menus, and signage, where other generators still garble lettering.

05

Precise local edits

Because the image is planned before it is painted, Canva says users can change one element without redrawing everything else around it.

06

Editable via Canva Magic

Craft's layered output can be turned into separate, editable design elements with Canva Magic, so text and objects stay tweakable after generation.

07

Developer API

Craft is exposed through the Canva Connect API image-generation endpoint as the model craft_v1_2k — pin it in POST /v1/image-generations to generate programmatically.

Pricing

What does it cost?

Canva has not published standalone pricing for Craft. Generations in Dream Lab count against your Canva plan's AI-use allowance (Free plan: up to 20 AI uses/month, per Canva's plan pages — snapshot October 2026). API pricing has not been announced.

Get started

How to use Canva Craft

1 · STEP

Open Dream Lab

Craft lives in Canva's Dream Lab tab — open it from the Canva homepage. It is available to all users, with generations counted against your plan's AI-use allowance.

2 · STEP

Brief it like a designer

Describe the layout, not just the subject: where the headline goes, what the hierarchy is, which text must appear verbatim. The planning stage works best with explicit layout instructions.

3 · STEP

Pick the aspect ratio first

Choose the format for the final use — Instagram post, banner, or slide — so the composition is built for it instead of cropped to fit.

4 · STEP

Proofread every letter

Canva's text claims are strong, but check spelling, prices, and dates in the image before you publish — a typo on a printed sign is your problem, not the model's.

FAQ

Canva Craft questions

What is Canva Craft?
Canva Craft is Canva's first image generation model built and trained in-house by Canva Research, released on October 22, 2026. It plans a design's layout before rendering it, and is trained on human-made design templates rather than photographs.
When was Canva Craft released?
Canva announced and released Craft on October 22, 2026, alongside developer access through the Canva Connect API.
How can I try Canva Craft?
Open the Dream Lab tab in Canva — it is live for all users. Developers can call it through the Canva Connect API image-generation endpoint with the model craft_v1_2k.
Is Canva Craft free?
Canva has not published standalone pricing for Craft. In Dream Lab, generations count against your Canva plan's AI-use allowance — the Free plan includes up to 20 AI uses per month, per Canva's plan pages (snapshot October 2026). No per-image Craft price has been announced.
How is Canva Craft different from models like GPT Image or Midjourney?
Craft is design-first: it plans layout before rendering and was trained on human-made design templates rather than photographs, which is why it targets legible text and editable compositions. General-purpose models still lead on photorealistic scenes — Craft's edge is marketing assets, posters, and social creatives.
What resolution and formats does Canva Craft support?
Up to 2K resolution and up to 30 aspect ratios, covering social, web, print, and presentation formats.
Can I use Canva Craft images commercially?
Per Canva's help pages, you own the images you create in Dream Lab as long as you follow Canva's Terms. Note that generated images carry a visible label showing they were AI-created.
What's coming next for Canva Craft?
Canva says two cheaper, faster variants — Craft-fast and Craft-ultrafast — are in development, and that image generation will reach third-party chatbots such as ChatGPT and Gemini via Canva's MCP server. No dates have been given for either.
