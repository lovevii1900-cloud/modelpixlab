The honest rule

When free is enough — and when it isn't

A simple decision rule, not a sales pitch.

Free is enough

Occasional use

A few images a month for posts, slides, or testing an idea. Start with the 3 free images, then a $4.9 pack that never expires — you only pay for what you actually generate.

Pay when

Volume or consistency

Daily generation, a brand style you return to, or client work at scale. Then a monthly plan ($9.9–$39.9/mo on ModelPixLab, or a dedicated subscription elsewhere) beats buying image by image.

Free is enough

Trying before committing

Never pay for a tool you have not generated with. If a tool has no free trial — Midjourney does not — try the same job on a free tier first and compare the output.

Pay when

One signature style

If your work lives inside one tool's look, the subscription buys you that style system plus capacity. That is the one case where paying monthly for a single tool makes sense.

Try it yourself

A two-minute free test

Do not pay for any tool before you have generated with it.

  1. Use ModelPixLab's 3 free images on a real job — a post, a slide, a thumbnail — not a throwaway test.
  2. If the result ships, a $4.9 pack that never expires covers your next jobs. If it does not, you have spent nothing and learned which model fits you.
  3. Only consider a monthly plan when you are generating often enough that a pack would run out every month.
