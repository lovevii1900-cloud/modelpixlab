FAQ

Questions, answered

What is GPT Image 2.5?
GPT Image 2.5 is OpenAI's image model released in September 2026. It comes in two versions — Flare for fast everyday generation and Sunburst for precision editing — and adds sketch-guided generation, templates, and multi-round editing that preserves details.
Is this GPT Image 2.5 generator free?
Yes. New accounts get 5 free images to try the tools, right in your browser. After that, paid plans add monthly credits and one-time credit packs never expire.
Which model should I choose, Flare or Sunburst?
Choose Flare when you want quick drafts and lots of variations. Choose Sunburst when you are editing a real photo or product image and details must stay exactly right.
Can it edit my existing photo?
Yes. Upload a photo and describe the change. GPT Image 2.5 is built to change only what you ask for while keeping faces, products and backgrounds intact across multiple edits.
What resolution can I download?
Up to 4K (3840×2160) in PNG, JPEG or WebP format.
Do I need an OpenAI or ChatGPT account?
No. This tool runs right in your browser — no OpenAI account, no ChatGPT subscription.
