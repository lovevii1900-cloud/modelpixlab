About ModelPixLab

One lab for every new AI image model

ModelPixLab is one lab for every new AI image model. Instead of juggling different apps and subscriptions, you get the newest image models — starting with GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2 — in a single, fast browser workspace.

What you can do

  • Generate images from text, or turn a rough sketch into a finished picture.
  • Edit real photos: swap backgrounds, restyle, and keep faces and details consistent across rounds.
  • Download results in up to 4K, ready for product pages, posts and posters.
  • Start free — new accounts get 5 free generations.

How it works

ModelPixLab is an independent tool interface built on official model APIs. You write a prompt, pick a model, and we route your request to the model provider and return the result. Read the step-by-step guide or see pricing.

Contact

Support and business inquiries: support@modelpixlab.com. We read everything and reply within a couple of business days.
