FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an OpenAI Images 2.6 sprite sheet into a GIF?

Generate a 3-column by 2-row sprite sheet in ChatGPT or the GPT-Image-2.6 API, drop the image into FrameGrid Studio above, adjust the frame rate (6–8 FPS works best for 6-frame loops), and click Compile & Download Looping .GIF.

Can I use this with 2×2 or 4×4 game sprite sheets from Midjourney or Nano Banana 2?

Yes. Change the Grid Topology selector at the top of the workbench to 2×2, 4×2, 3×3, or 4×4 to slice any uniform sprite sheet into an animated GIF.
